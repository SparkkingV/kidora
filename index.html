WHY KIDORA

Technology that supports therapy.

KIDORA brings sensor-based monitoring, therapy activities and progress tracking together in one connected platform.

Smart Monitoring

ESP32-based devices can capture movement and activity data during therapy sessions.

Progress Tracking

Therapists can monitor activity performance and observe changes over multiple sessions.

Therapist Dashboard

A dedicated professional dashboard provides therapists with a centralized view of their assigned children.

Child-Friendly

A simple and engaging interface makes therapy activities easier for children to understand and follow.

Real-Time Data

Sensor information can be transmitted to the KIDORA platform and displayed without manually refreshing.

Web & Mobile

KIDORA is designed for computers, tablets and smartphones, with an Android app planned.

HOW IT WORKS

From movement to meaningful insight.

KIDORA connects the child's activity, smart sensors and occupational therapist through one real-time system.

01

Activity

The child performs a therapy activity under the guidance of the therapist.

02

Sense

Sensors connected to the ESP32 capture movement and activity information.

03

Sync

The ESP32 sends the collected data securely to the KIDORA backend.

04

Understand

The therapist sees the activity data and uses it to understand progress.

THE TECHNOLOGY

One connected therapy ecosystem.

KIDORA combines embedded sensors, wireless connectivity, cloud services, realtime data and role-based web applications.

ESP32 Device Captures therapy activity data
Sensor Network Measures movement and interaction
Cloud Backend Stores and processes therapy data
Role-Based Access Separate child and therapist portals
